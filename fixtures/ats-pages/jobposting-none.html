Field Service Technician

Meridian Elevators services lifts and escalators in twelve cities. As a field service technician you will handle scheduled maintenance and emergency call-outs in the Manchester area.

Requirements

  • NVQ Level 3 in lift engineering or equivalent
  • Full UK driving licence

Benefits

  • Company van
  • Overtime paid at time and a half

Pay is £38,000 per year with a call-out allowance.
